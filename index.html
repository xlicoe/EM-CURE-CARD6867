Classroom Card

EM-CURE: Funding the Edge - Crafting Winning Grant Proposals for Edge AI Innovations

Class Structure

Week 1: The Landscape & The Hook

  • Session 1 (The Funding Landscape): Introduction to NSF, DoL, and industry fellowships. Goal mapping.
  • Session 2 (Decoding the RFP): Workshop on reading a Request for Proposal (RFP). Identifying eligibility and budget constraints.
  • Session 3 (The Heilmeier Catechism): Structuring the project narrative and articulating the "elevator pitch".
  • Session 4 (Visualizing the Vision): Translating edge architectures into schematic figures (PowerPoint, Inkscape, BioRender).

Week 2: Structuring & Execution

  • Session 5 (Reverse-Engineering the Outline): Mapping exact RFP instructions to a document hierarchy.
  • Session 6 (Project Management): Introduction to Gantt charts and realistic prototyping phases.
  • Session 7 (Drafting the Core): Writing "Intellectual Merit" and "Broader Impacts". Embedding figures.
  • Session 8 (Peer Review Panel): Mock review panel scoring outlines against RFP criteria.
